## 💡 Engineering Principles

<details>
<summary>Read my principles</summary>

<br />

### 01 — Simple beats clever

If the solution can be understood quickly, that's usually a good sign.

### 02 — Architecture serves the product

Don't build complexity just because the technology allows it.

### 03 — Reusable ≠ over-engineered

Build abstractions when repetition and change actually justify them.

### 04 — Measure before optimizing

Performance improvements should be based on real problems and real measurements.

### 05 — Ship early

A real product in users' hands teaches more than a perfect product sitting locally.

### 06 — Keep learning

Technology changes. The ability to understand and adapt matters more.

</details>
